- Full generated mirror HTML, media libraries, backups, `node_modules`, `.git`, `web/dist`, `.astro` and files over 1 MB were not copied into this branch.
- Large generated mirror files are represented above by metadata and SHA-256 only.

## Files That Could Not Be Fully Compared

- Full generated mirror pages were not diffed against the live site because they are generated output and many are larger than 1 MB.
- Broad recursive scans of all `Documents` and the entire old `matt AI` tree were stopped because they were too slow/noisy; targeted known project paths and Codex summaries were used instead.
- Stash file entries are preserved as Git stash content and summarized with SHA-256 hashes of the stash text content where available.
