Everyone Is Welcome

Building everyday lifesavers on Koh Samui

Koh Samui Surf Lifesaving Club brings people together around a simple mission: safer beaches and stronger community. Inspired by Australian surf club traditions, the club is shaped for island life in Thailand.

Whether you want to patrol, learn first aid, support youth programs, sponsor equipment, or simply help create safer beach days, there is a place for you here.

Life Saving

Patrol skills, rescue confidence and practical beach safety

Volunteer lifesavers are trained to observe conditions, provide safety advice, assist swimmers, respond to incidents and support first aid. The club's role is to grow those skills locally so Samui has more people ready to help when it matters.

Club Programs

How members can get involved

Beach Patrols

Volunteer patrol teams support safer beach days with water safety, first aid awareness and practical local knowledge.

Rescue Readiness

Club training builds confidence around rescue boards, tube rescues, radios, observation and emergency response.

Community Education

Visitors, schools and families can learn simple surf safety habits before they step into unfamiliar water.

Training Pathways

From first skills to active lifesaver

Training follows the spirit of recognised surf lifesaving pathways, adapted for Koh Samui's community and available conditions.

  • Surf Rescue Certificate pathway for confident swimmers aged 13+
  • Bronze Medallion preparation for active patrol members
  • First aid, CPR and resuscitation practice sessions
  • Board, tube and beach response skills for island conditions

Nippers & Youth

Confident kids, safer families, stronger beaches

Nippers-style sessions help children understand waves, flags, currents, boards, teamwork and beach respect in a fun, supportive environment.

Surf Sports

Board paddling, beach sprints, swimming confidence and friendly club challenges give members goals to train for together.

FAQ

Surf club questions

Who can join the club?

Locals, expats, visitors and families are welcome. New members can help with patrols, training, fundraising, events, youth programs or practical club support.

Do I need previous lifesaving experience?

No. Confident swimmers can start with entry-level training, while experienced lifesavers can help shape patrol systems, mentoring and emergency readiness.

How can businesses support the club?

Businesses can sponsor equipment, support training days, host fundraisers, provide prizes, or help cover costs for safety gear and youth participation.
