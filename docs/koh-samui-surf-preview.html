Surf Life Saving Club · Koh Samui

Teaching local kids to be safe in the ocean

Most local children begin with no ability to put their face in the water. SLSC Koh Samui runs structured junior water safety sessions: ocean swimming, CPR, rip awareness and board skills, prioritising Thai and expat children.

Sessions already run every Sunday at Nature Bar, Mae Nam, based on the Australian SLSC model. The foundation is live. The gap is scale, equipment and facilities.

Pathway to Lifeguards

Skills that can lead to future employment

Samui Surf Life Saving Club is embedding structured ocean safety and lifesaving training into its core model, designed specifically for local Thai and expat children. Regular sessions can help young people progress toward future roles as lifeguards and water-safety professionals.

Our Impact

Youth Lifesaver Employment Pathway

Ocean swimming, CPR and practical lifesaving skills help children become confident, capable young people with real pathways into water-safety work.

Minairal Collaboration

Participating children have access to clean drinking water during sessions as part of a pilot program to reduce plastic bottles across the club and schools.

Coaches and Volunteers

Local and expat volunteers can help coach, supervise, fundraise and support safe Sunday sessions at Nature Bar.
