Life Saving

Patrol skills, rescue confidence and practical beach safety

The club can grow a local patrol culture for busy beach days, public holidays, family events and junior programs. Members learn observation, communication, rescue awareness, first aid support and clear beach safety messaging.

The training pathway follows the familiar surf club model: start with water safety basics, progress into rescue awards, then keep skills fresh through regular practice.

Surf Rescue Certificate

Entry-level rescue skills for confident swimmers aged 13 and above, building towards patrol readiness.

Bronze Medallion Pathway

A future core award pathway for members ready to take on stronger lifesaving and patrol responsibilities.

Additional Awards

CPR, first aid, advanced resuscitation, rescue craft support and patrol leadership can be added as the club grows.

Nippers

Sunday Nippers for beach awareness, confidence and fun

Nippers gives children a structured way to learn beach safety, ocean swimming, flags, boards, teamwork and respect for changing conditions. Sessions are built around age groups, parent involvement and safe rotations between beach and water activities.

  • Age groups and swim ability checks before water activities
  • Age managers and assistant volunteers for each group
  • Beach drills, water safety, games, relays and confidence building
  • Parent or guardian presence on the beach during sessions

Surf Sports

Training that keeps lifesaving skills sharp

Surf sports gives members a reason to train regularly and turns practical lifesaving skills into goals. The Samui program can include ocean swimming, board paddling, beach sprinting, relays, pool rescue practice and friendly club challenges.

The pathway can support junior athletes, open members and masters, while helping future lifesavers build fitness, discipline and confidence.

A strong surf sports culture helps create stronger lifesavers.
