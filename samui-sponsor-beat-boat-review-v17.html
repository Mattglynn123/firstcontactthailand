Anzac Day 2026

A Dawn Service On The Water

A huge thank you to Beat Boat, who supported our club's Anzac Day on Koh Samui 2026.

25 people gathered at 5:30am for a truly special dawn service aboard a beautiful boat, generously donated by The Beat Boat.

As the sun rose over the ocean, the sound of the waves combined with Anzac music created a powerful and moving atmosphere, offering a moment to reflect on what the Anzacs may have experienced landing at Gallipoli in 1915.

The boat looked incredible, proudly displaying Australian and New Zealand flags.
