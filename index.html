Life Saving

Training young people to be safe in the ocean

The club is built around practical beach and ocean skills: swimming confidence, rescue awareness, board skills, CPR, rip awareness and teamwork. The goal is a professional, structured pathway that can grow with the island.

Nippers

Sunday sessions for confident kids

A friendly entry point for children who can already swim: ocean swimming, beach games, lifesaving skills and water-safety habits in a club environment.
