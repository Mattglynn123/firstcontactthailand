Anzac Day 2026 Support

A huge thank you to Bloom Samui, who supported Anzac Day on Koh Samui 2026.

Partnership

Wreath Support For Anzac Day

25 people gathered at 5:30am for a truly special dawn service aboard a beautiful boat, generously donated by The Beat Boat Koh Samui.

After returning to shore, we held a 9am beach service at Nature Bar, where the Thai owner warmly welcomed everyone and carried out a beautiful wreath, generously donated by Bloom Samui.
