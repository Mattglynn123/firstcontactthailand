# Phuket Charity Programs

We support several Phuket charities, primarily via the Rotary Patong Club (primarily supporting children, safety, emergency response, education, and local community wellbeing.).

We also work with many other charity groups and would love to hear from others so we can support your charity.

[

## Save A Child Swim Program

Supporting basic swimming and water-survival skills for local school children.



](https://www.rotarypatong.org/13774/Page/Show?ClassCode=Page&Slug=save-a-child-swim-program-1)[

## Safe Roads Project

Improving road safety with safer crossings, U-turns, lights, and road-safety upgrades.



](https://www.rotarypatong.org/page/safe-roads-project)[

## Rescue Boat Patong Beach

Supporting Patong lifeguards with rescue capability and emergency sea-safety response.



](https://www.rotarypatong.org/page/rescue-boat-patong)[

## Tsunami Scholarship Program

Helping students with ongoing education support after the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami.



](https://www.rotarypatong.org/page/scholarship-program)[

## AED Pads

Supporting rapid-response emergency care with AED equipment and replacement pads.



](https://www.rotarypatong.org/page/aed-pads)
