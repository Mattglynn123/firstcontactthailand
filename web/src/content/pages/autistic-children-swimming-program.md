Support water confidence, safety skills, and patient learning for autistic children and their families.

## Water Confidence

The program helps children build confidence in and around the water.

Sessions are supportive, calm, and encouraging.

## Safe Swimming

Children learn practical swimming skills and water safety awareness.

The focus is progress at each child's pace.

## Active Learning

Swimming creates positive movement, structure, and confidence-building moments.

The program supports learning through patient practice.
