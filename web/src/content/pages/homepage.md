FIRST CONTACT THAILAND

## About Us

First Contact was founded in Koh Samui in 2024 with a clear purpose: to bring locals, expats, and travellers together by connecting them with trusted services and reliable local businesses.

Supporting local charities, Rotary clubs, and community groups across Thailand is a major focus for First Contact and remains at the heart of everything we do.

Our collective network began with more than 500 businesses in Koh Samui and has since expanded on a large scale across Thailand.

## How It Works

Membership with First Contact is free. Our members give back by donating a percentage of the revenue they generate through First Contact to charitable and community causes.

We can direct you to reliable providers across a wide range of sectors, including marketing and advertising, legal services, tours and entertainment, food and beverage deals, vehicle hire, accommodation, property development and management, and many more.

First Contact helps people find the right services while supporting the local communities we all care about.
