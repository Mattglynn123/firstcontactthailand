# Chiang Mai Charity Programs

We support Chiang Mai charity projects, primarily via the Chiang Mai International Rotary Club, including children, education, health, water safety, community wellbeing and practical support programs.

We also work with many other charity groups and would love to hear from others so we can support your charity.

[![Children's Water Safety and Drowning Prevention](/assets/fct/chiang-mai-water-safety.jpg)](https://www.cmirotary.org/children-s-water-safety-program)

## Children's Water Safety and Drowning Prevention

Supporting water survival training for local students, helping children build basic swimming and ocean-safety skills.

[Read more](https://www.cmirotary.org/children-s-water-safety-program)

[![BCMF B.K. Kee Patient House](/assets/fct/chiang-mai-patient-house.jpg)](https://www.cmirotary.org/bcmf-bk-kee-patient-house)

## BCMF B.K. Kee Patient House

Helping provide support and accommodation for patients and families who travel to Chiang Mai for medical treatment.

[Read more](https://www.cmirotary.org/bcmf-bk-kee-patient-house)

[![B.E.A.M. GED Program](/assets/fct/chiang-mai-beam-ged.jpg)](https://www.cmirotary.org/beam-foundation-ged-program)

## B.E.A.M. GED Program

Supporting education pathways and opportunities for young people through GED learning and wider community education.

[Read more](https://www.cmirotary.org/beam-foundation-ged-program)

[![School Vision Screening](/assets/fct/chiang-mai-vision-screening.jpg)](https://www.cmirotary.org/school-vision-screening)

## School Vision Screening

Screening school children for eyesight issues and helping children access spectacles so poor vision does not hold back learning.

[Read more](https://www.cmirotary.org/school-vision-screening)

[![Cultivating a Peace Culture in School](/assets/fct/chiang-mai-peace-culture.jpg)](https://www.cmirotary.org/cultivating-peace-culture-in-school)

## Cultivating a Peace Culture in School

Helping students build confidence, discipline and non-violent conflict resolution through peace-culture education.

[Read more](https://www.cmirotary.org/cultivating-peace-culture-in-school)

## Winter Clothing and School Supplies Appeal

Collecting and distributing winter clothing and school supplies for communities that need practical seasonal support.

[Read more](https://www.cmirotary.org/winter-clothing-school-supplies-app)
