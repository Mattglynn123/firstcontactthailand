# Move to Thailand Guide

A simple starting point for planning your move to Thailand, with the key areas to check before you relocate, rent, buy, work, retire, or settle into local life.

## Visas & Residency

Start by understanding which visa route fits your plans, including retirement, family, education, work, investment, or long-stay options.

## Where to Live

Thailand offers very different lifestyles across islands, beach towns, cities, and mountain areas. Think about schools, transport, healthcare, work, community, and day-to-day costs.

## Property & Rentals

Before renting or buying, check ownership rules, contracts, deposits, land titles, management fees, and local legal support.

## Healthcare & Insurance

Review medical insurance, emergency access, hospitals nearby, medication needs, and support for children or older family members.

## Banking & Money

Plan how you will handle banking, transfers, tax questions, income, pensions, and currency exchange.

## Trusted Local Support

Moving is easier with reliable local contacts for legal help, property, removals, vehicles, insurance, schools, healthcare, business support, and community introductions.
