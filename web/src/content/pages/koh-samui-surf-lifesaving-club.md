Support local ocean safety and lifesaving education for children and families on Samui.

## Support The Program

Help the club keep practical ocean safety training available for local children and families.

Every bit of support helps the program reach more kids.

## Ocean Awareness

Beach awareness and lifesaving education give families more confidence around the water.

The goal is safer days on Samui beaches.

## Safety Skills

Children learn swimming confidence, ocean safety, and practical skills they can use.

The program builds stronger swimmers and safer habits.
