# Bangkok Charity Programs

We support Bangkok charity programs, including projects run by the Rotary Club of Bangkok Foundation.

[![Water and sanitation project by Rotary Club of Bangkok Foundation](https://rcbfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/water1-300x300.jpg)

## Clean Drinking Water Systems

Supporting clean drinking water systems, sanitation, and hygiene education for rural schools and communities.



](https://rcbfoundation.org/projects/improve-sanitation-hygiene-and-clean-drinking-water-facilities-in-needy-schools-and-communities/)[![Rotary nursing scholarship project](https://rcbfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/nurse-home-2-300x300.jpg)

## Kid’s Day Out

Rotary nursing scholarships help students in financial hardship and support Thailand’s need for registered nurses.



](https://rcbfoundation.org/projects/open-opportunities-for-rotary-nursing-scholars/)[![Children of the Forest project supported by Rotary Club of Bangkok Foundation](https://rcbfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/COF3-300x300.jpg)

## Polio Plus

Helping stateless children access preparatory education, Thai language support, and safer pathways into school.



](https://rcbfoundation.org/projects/peace-and-conflict-resolution-by-supporting-children-of-the-forest-sangklaburi/)[![Protect the Environment project by Rotary Club of Bangkok Foundation](https://rcbfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Protect-the-environment5-300x225.jpg)

## Low Income Communities

Supporting waste-management learning, sorting bins, composting, and reusable water bottles at a rural school.



](https://rcbfoundation.org/projects/protect-the-environment-by-implementing-waste-management-learning-and-infrastructures-in-a-rural-school-to-reduce-plastic-waste/)[![Youth charity support for children in Bangkok](https://rotarybangkoksouth.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/new-kids.jpg)

## Youth

Choose the Rotary category you prefer, or let the foundation allocate support where it is most helpful.



](https://rcbfoundation.org/product/you-pick/)
