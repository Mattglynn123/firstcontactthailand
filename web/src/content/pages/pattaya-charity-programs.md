# Pattaya Charity Programs

We support several Pattaya charity projects, primarily via the Rotary Club of Pattaya.

[![Young Entrepreneurs Fair](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1559027615-cd4628902d4a?auto=format&fit=crop&w=900&q=80)

## Young Entrepreneurs Fair

Supporting young people with practical enterprise experience and community-led opportunities.



](https://www.rotarypattaya.com/young-enterpreneurs-fair/)[![Rotary Drinking Water](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1541976844346-f18aeac57b06?auto=format&fit=crop&w=900&q=80)

## Rotary Drinking Water

Helping provide access to safe drinking water and practical health support for local communities.



](https://www.rotarypattaya.com/rotary-drinking-water/)[![COVID Protection](https://firstcontactthailand.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/pattaya-covid-protection.webp)

## COVID Protection

Community protection work supporting safer local health outcomes.



](https://www.rotarypattaya.com/covid-protection/)[![Promoting Peace](https://firstcontactthailand.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/pattaya-promoting-peace.webp)

## Promoting Peace

Children use art to express peace, with proceeds supporting scholarships and local Rotary charities.



](https://www.rotarypattaya.com/promoting-peace/)[![Shop Local Too](https://firstcontactthailand.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/pattaya-shop-local-too.webp)

## Shop Local Too!

Encouraging local support and community connection through Rotary Pattaya initiatives.



](https://www.rotarypattaya.com/shop-local-too/)[![Save a Childs Eyes](https://firstcontactthailand.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/pattaya-save-a-childs-eyes.webp)

## Save a Childs Eyes

Providing eye tests and glasses for underprivileged children who need help to learn at school.



](https://www.rotarypattaya.com/save-a-childs-eyes/)[![LIVE LOVE program](https://firstcontactthailand.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/pattaya-live-love-program.webp)

## LIVE LOVE program

Helping vulnerable children and carers build confidence, healthy choices, and happier futures.



](https://www.rotarypattaya.com/live-love-program/)
