# Hua Hin Charity Programs

We support several Hua Hin charities, primarily via the Rotary Royal Hua Hin (primarily supporting children, education, health, lifesaving skills, and community wellbeing).

We also work with many other charity groups and would love to hear from others so we can support your charity.

[

## Hua Hin Youth Football Championship for Literacy

Supporting youth sport, literacy, teamwork, and school community participation in Hua Hin.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/hua-hin-youth-football-championship-for-literacy-2026-rotary-club-of-royal-hua-hin-hua-hin-city-municipal-stadium-3-may-2026)[

## Ultrasound Technology Project

Helping hospitals improve diagnostic capability through new ultrasound technology and Rotary support.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/service-project-handover-ultrasound-technology-singburi-hospital-19-april-2026)[

## Police Help Save Lives

AED donation and lifesaving training for Hua Hin Police Department first responders.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/police-help-save-lives-service-project-aed-donation-and-training-hua-hin-police-department-9-april-2026)[

## World Water Day Service Project

Providing drinking water support for local government and community service teams during the hot season.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/service-project-handover-world-water-day-22-march-2026)[

## Children Of The Forest

Supporting education, healthcare, and child protection for migrant and stateless children and families.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/service-project-handover-children-of-the-forest-18-19-march-2026)[

## Library Books For Local Schools

Promoting education and literacy with Thai storybooks and dictionaries for school libraries.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/service-project-handover-160-library-books-baan-nong-krathing-school-9-march-2026)[

## Lifesaving Training For Students

Hands-on CPR, AED, and choking response training for students in the Hua Hin region.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/lifesaving-training-for-students-silpakorn-university-7-march-2026)[

## University Social Responsibility

Student-led service work creating practical support and positive experiences for local children.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/university-social-responsibility-rotaract-club-of-stamford-international-university-6-february-2026)[

## Eyeglasses For Children

Providing prescription eyeglasses for children through school support and local partner initiatives.



](https://rotaryroyalhuahin.org/Stories/eyeglasses-for-children-wangklaikangwon-school-14-january-2026)[

## Water Safety And Drowning Prevention

Training children in practical water safety and drowning prevention skills.
